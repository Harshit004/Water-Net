IoT Platform
Every drop, tracked
in real time.
WATERNET gives enterprises complete visibility over their water dispensing infrastructure — from water quality sensors to usage analytics — on one unified dashboard.
Wi-Fi Connected Dispensers
Each WATERNET machine connects over Wi-Fi and streams sensor data to the cloud in real time, no manual readings required.
Multi-Sensor Monitoring
Track water temperature, TDS (Total Dissolved Solids), pH level, total purified output, and raw water consumed — all live.
Usage Analytics
Understand daily and weekly consumption trends, purification efficiency ratios, and filter replacement schedules with visual charts.
Smart Alerts
Receive automatic alerts when TDS exceeds safe thresholds, pH drops, machines go offline, or filters need replacement.
Multi-Client Portal
Enterprise clients each get their own isolated dashboard with secure login — perfect for corporates, hospitals, campuses and hotels.
Sustainability Reporting
Automatically calculate plastic bottles saved and CO₂ emissions reduced — share impact reports with stakeholders in one click.
Admin Console
Full admin panel for the WATERNET team to manage all clients, machines, user accounts, and system-wide alert thresholds.
Mobile Friendly
Responsive design works on any device. Facility managers can check machine status on their phone from anywhere.
Easy Machine Onboarding
Register a new dispenser in under two minutes. Enter the serial number and location — the machine connects automatically over Wi-Fi.
